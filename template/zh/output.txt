输出格式如下：

{
  "source": "WORD_TO_BE_REPLACED",
  "distractor1": "WORD_YOU_CHOOSE_1",
  "distractor2": "WORD_YOU_CHOOSE_2",
  "distractor3": "WORD_YOU_CHOOSE_3"

- 不要输出任何说明文字。
